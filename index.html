Ambassador’s desk

India and the Republic of Congo: Warm and Friendly Relations

Mr. Madan-Lal Raigar assumed charge as the Ambassador of India to the Republic of Congo on 20 October 2023. He is an officer of the Indian Foreign Service of 2007 and, before this assignment, served as Director in the Development Partnership Administration Division of the Ministry of External Affairs, New Delhi.

His previous assignments include Consul General of India in Hamburg, and Chargé d’Affaires a.i. at the Embassy of India in Antananarivo. He has also served in Khartoum, Vancouver, Colombo, Abu Dhabi and Jeddah.

India and the Republic of Congo enjoy warm and friendly relations dating back to the pre-independence era of the ROC. India opened its diplomatic Mission in Brazzaville in November 2019.
